# Surface: docs/index.html

Scope: the whole product, one page. Mode: Operate.
Audience/job: one non-technical woman in her late fifties scanning ranked office-job ads in the province of Treviso, opening the good ones and marking them. Constraints: static page, data from annunci.json, statuses in localStorage, Italian, large text, works at 200% zoom.
Memorable moment: every card says in plain words why it fits her, before she clicks.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The standard job-board list (Indeed/InfoJobs), played straight for one reader: ranked ad cards that answer "vale la pena?" before the click. Refuses dashboards, filter sidebars, search boxes and anything she must learn.
OWN-WORLD: Cool light-gray page ground, white cards with a 1px gray border and 8px radius, near-black text, one deep trust-blue accent for links and the primary button, green fit label, amber warning label; Segoe UI/system sans, 19px body, tight 1.2 scale.
STORY: She opens the shortcut, sees when results were updated, reads the top ads and their reason, opens one in a new tab, applies, marks it. New results appear by themselves every morning.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar: title left; update time right (no search button: the user removed it). Under it three tabs with counts. Then the first cards in a centered 860px column.
FORM: canon (standing exit chosen by the user), Indeed/InfoJobs craft level; seed key 2b59481c.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
